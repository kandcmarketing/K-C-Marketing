Data compiled from public OIG enforcement listings and the Advisory Opinion browse index. Enforcement category assignments reflect a keyword-based classifier applied to the action title and OIG's one-sentence summary; cases implicating multiple theories (e.g. AKS + unlicensed provider) fall into the highest-precedence category. Provider type classifications are similarly keyword-based.

Advisory Opinion arrangement type and requestor industry classifications are manual (each opinion reviewed individually). The statutes implicated tags reflect what each opinion analyzes directly; note that every OIG advisory opinion implicates AKS by default, so the tag is only analytically useful when combined with other statutes (most notably the Beneficiary Inducement CMP). OIG does not have jurisdiction to opine on the physician self-referral law (Stark); Stark enforcement sits with CMS.

Each row links to the underlying OIG page (click the ↗ in the Src column). Exclusion-only actions are shown with a $0 amount and excluded from the dollar chart but counted in the frequency chart. In each CMP case resolved through settlement agreement, the settling party has contested OIG's allegations and denied any liability; no CMP judgment or finding of liability has been made.
